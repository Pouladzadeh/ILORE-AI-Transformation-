What is ILORE Academy?

ILORE Academy is the AI education division of ILORE. Its purpose is to build shared AI capability across an organization so that AI becomes a practical business capability rather than an isolated software tool. Academy programs are designed for both the people who use AI every day and the leaders who decide how it is adopted.

Academy is the Learn phase of ILORE's three-phase system: Learn (Academy), Implement (Transform), and Grow (Discover). Organizations typically start here to build the knowledge and confidence that responsible AI adoption requires before moving into implementation.

What programs does ILORE Academy offer?

ILORE Academy offers three groups of programs. Core AI programs build foundational, shared AI literacy across teams: AI Fundamentals, Generative AI, Prompt Engineering, and Agentic AI.

Workplace AI programs are applied programs for everyday work, leadership, and responsible adoption: Microsoft Copilot, AI Productivity, Executive AI Leadership, and Responsible AI.

Sector Programs are tailored to the realities of regulated, public-facing sectors: AI for Healthcare, AI for Education, and AI for Government.

Who is ILORE Academy for?

ILORE Academy is for organizations that want their workforce and leadership to use AI confidently and responsibly. Core AI programs suit teams that need a common baseline of AI literacy; Workplace AI programs suit people applying AI in daily work and executives leading adoption; Sector Programs suit healthcare, education, and government organizations whose regulated, public-facing context shapes how AI can be used.

How does Academy connect to Transform and Discover?

Academy is the starting point of ILORE's AI transformation system. Once an organization has built AI capability through Academy, ILORE Transform turns AI opportunities into working systems — from an AI Opportunity Assessment through strategy, automation, agents, and custom solutions — and ILORE Discover improves how the organization is found across search and AI-powered discovery.
