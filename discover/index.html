What is ILORE Discover?

ILORE Discover is the division of ILORE that improves an organization's visibility across search and AI-powered discovery. Its work starts from an organization's own knowledge and builds outward through three layers: a knowledge infrastructure foundation, traditional search optimization, and AI-powered visibility on assistants and answer engines.

Discover is the Grow phase of ILORE's three-phase system — Learn, Implement, Grow — and applies the same AI capability an organization has built internally to how it is found externally.

What services does ILORE Discover provide?

ILORE Discover's services are organized in three layers.

  • Knowledge Infrastructure (foundation): Knowledge Graph Development, Structured Data Implementation, and AI-Friendly Website Optimization.
  • Search Optimization (traditional search): SEO and Content Strategy.
  • AI-Powered Visibility (AI discovery): AEO, GEO, and AI Search Optimization.

What are AEO, GEO and AI search optimization?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) are the practices of making an organization's content easy for AI assistants and answer engines to find, understand, and cite. AI search optimization is the broader discipline that covers both, alongside the structured data and knowledge infrastructure that AI systems rely on. ILORE Discover delivers these as a complement to traditional SEO, not a replacement for it.

Why does knowledge infrastructure come first?

Knowledge infrastructure comes first because both search engines and AI assistants understand an organization through structured, machine-readable knowledge. Knowledge graph development, structured data implementation, and AI-friendly website optimization make an organization's expertise legible to those systems, which is the foundation that search optimization and AI-powered visibility then build on.
