What is ILORE Transform?

ILORE Transform is the division of ILORE that implements AI inside organizations. Where ILORE Academy builds capability and ILORE Discover builds visibility, Transform turns AI opportunities into working systems: AI readiness, strategy, automation, intelligent agents, and custom AI solutions.

Transform is the Implement phase of ILORE's three-phase system — Learn, Implement, Grow — and is where AI becomes part of daily operations, decision-making, customer engagement, and long-term strategy.

What are the five ILORE Transform services?

ILORE Transform provides five services, ordered from understanding readiness to running solutions:

  • AI Opportunity Assessment — evaluate organizational AI maturity and identify opportunities.
  • AI Strategy Development — develop enterprise AI roadmaps aligned with business objectives.
  • AI Process Automation — automate repetitive business processes using AI technologies.
  • AI Agent Development — design intelligent assistants that improve productivity and decision-making.
  • Custom AI Solutions — develop tailored AI applications addressing specific organizational needs.

Where does an engagement with ILORE Transform begin?

An engagement with ILORE Transform usually begins with an AI Opportunity Assessment. The assessment evaluates an organization's AI maturity and identifies where AI can create value, and its findings inform which of the other services — strategy, automation, agents, or custom solutions — comes next. Organizations request an assessment through a guided four-step intake on ilore.ai.

How does Transform relate to Academy and Discover?

ILORE Transform sits between the other two divisions. ILORE Academy prepares the workforce and leadership with the AI knowledge that implementation requires, and ILORE Discover ensures that an organization is found and understood across search and AI-powered discovery once its AI capability is in place. Together the three divisions form one AI transformation system.
