What are Custom AI Solutions?

Custom AI Solutions are AI applications developed for a specific organization and a specific need, rather than adopted off the shelf. A custom AI solution is designed around the organization's own data, workflows, and objectives, and built to fit how the organization actually works.

Within ILORE, Custom AI Solutions is the fifth of ILORE Transform's five services, and the one that addresses needs the other four — assessment, strategy, process automation, and agents — do not cover on their own.

When does an organization need a custom AI solution?

An organization needs a custom AI solution when its requirement is specific enough that standard AI tools do not meet it: a workflow unique to the organization, a decision that depends on its own data, or a customer engagement need that general-purpose products do not address. An AI Opportunity Assessment or an AI strategy typically identifies when a custom solution is the right path.

What does ILORE's Custom AI Solutions service involve?

ILORE's Custom AI Solutions service develops a tailored AI application for the organizational need identified. The solution is designed for responsible adoption and measurable business value, and it is built to become part of the organization's operations, decision-making, customer engagement, or long-term strategy rather than an isolated tool.

How do Custom AI Solutions fit with ILORE's other services?

Custom AI Solutions complete ILORE Transform's set of services. Where AI Process Automation and AI Agent Development apply established patterns — automated processes and intelligent assistants — Custom AI Solutions address what those patterns do not cover. ILORE Academy prepares the workforce to use what is built, and ILORE Discover ensures the organization is found across search and AI-powered discovery.
