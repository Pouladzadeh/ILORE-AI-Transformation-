What is AI Process Automation?

AI Process Automation is the use of AI technologies to automate repetitive business processes. Unlike conventional automation, which follows fixed rules, AI process automation can handle work that involves language, documents, and variable inputs — the kind of recurring tasks that previously needed a person for every instance.

Within ILORE, AI Process Automation is the third of ILORE Transform's five services and is one of the main ways AI becomes part of an organization's daily operations.

What does ILORE's AI Process Automation service involve?

ILORE's AI Process Automation service applies AI technologies to an organization's repetitive business processes. The processes to automate are typically identified through an AI Opportunity Assessment or an AI strategy, and the automation is designed to fit the organization's actual way of working — with responsible adoption and measurable business value as the goals.

Which processes are candidates for AI automation?

Candidates for AI process automation are business processes that are repetitive, high in volume, and involve information that people currently handle manually — for example, processing documents and requests, routing and responding to routine communications, or preparing recurring reports. An AI Opportunity Assessment helps an organization identify which of its own processes fit.

How does AI Process Automation relate to AI agents?

AI Process Automation and AI Agent Development are complementary ILORE Transform services. Process automation handles defined, repetitive processes end to end. AI agents are intelligent assistants that work alongside people to improve productivity and decision-making. Many organizations use both: automated processes for routine work, and agents for work that still needs a person in the loop.
