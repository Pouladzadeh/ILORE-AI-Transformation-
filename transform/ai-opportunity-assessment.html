What is an AI Opportunity Assessment?

An AI Opportunity Assessment is an evaluation of an organization's AI maturity and the opportunities where AI can create value. ILORE uses it to understand how far an organization has moved from AI experimentation toward practical use, and to identify where AI could become part of daily operations, decision-making, customer engagement, or long-term strategy.

It is the first of ILORE Transform's five services and the entry point to the others: AI Strategy Development, AI Process Automation, AI Agent Development, and Custom AI Solutions.

How does the AI Opportunity Assessment work?

The assessment begins with a guided intake on ilore.ai. In four steps, an organization describes itself, where it is with AI today, the challenges it is trying to solve, and how to get in touch. ILORE uses this information to better understand the organization's AI maturity and potential opportunities, and follows up with the organization directly.

Who should request an AI Opportunity Assessment?

An AI Opportunity Assessment suits any organization that wants to move AI beyond experimentation and into the way it actually works. That includes organizations that have not yet adopted AI and want to know where to start, organizations that have experimented with AI tools without measurable results, and organizations with clear ambitions that need an implementation path.

What happens after the assessment?

The findings of an AI Opportunity Assessment point to the next ILORE service. An organization that needs an enterprise-level plan moves to AI Strategy Development; one with repetitive processes to automate moves to AI Process Automation; one that needs intelligent assistants moves to AI Agent Development; and one with a specific need moves to Custom AI Solutions. Where the workforce needs capability first, ILORE Academy programs come in alongside.
