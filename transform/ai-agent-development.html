What is AI Agent Development?

AI Agent Development is the design of intelligent assistants, known as AI agents, that improve productivity and decision-making. An AI agent is an AI system that can understand requests, use an organization's information and tools, and carry out or support tasks on behalf of the people it works with.

Within ILORE, AI Agent Development is the fourth of ILORE Transform's five services, and it connects to ILORE Academy's Agentic AI program, which builds the workforce's understanding of how agents work.

What does ILORE's AI Agent Development service involve?

ILORE's AI Agent Development service designs intelligent assistants for an organization's specific context: the people they support, the knowledge they need access to, and the productivity or decision-making they are meant to improve. Agents are designed for responsible adoption — with people remaining in the loop for decisions — and for measurable business value.

What can an AI agent do for an organization?

An AI agent can act as an intelligent assistant for a team or function: answering questions from an organization's own knowledge, helping staff complete recurring tasks faster, supporting customer engagement, and surfacing information that improves decisions. The right agents for an organization are identified through an AI Opportunity Assessment or an AI strategy.

How do AI agents differ from process automation?

AI agents and AI process automation are complementary. AI Process Automation handles repetitive business processes end to end without a person in each instance. AI agents are assistants that work with people, improving their productivity and decision-making on work that still needs human judgment. Organizations often combine both.
